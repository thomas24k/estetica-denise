3. The colour rule

Black carries. Gold signs. Orange acts.

Black #15110E
Off-white #F6F4F1
Logo gold #CBB054
Deep gold #7D5F10
Sunset #FF6A2B

Black and off-white are the two grounds, like her sign on her wall. Gold is the logo and the big headings on black, or a filled shape. Never small gold text on white (it cannot be read); there I use the deep gold. Sunset orange is only two things: the booking button and an offer price. If a screen shows orange anywhere else, it is a mistake. Orange never sits directly on gold; black or white goes between them. Client photos keep their own colours (red, lilac, cherry nails) and are the only other colour on the page.

5. Test script for five real clients

Give her the phone with the page open. Do not help. Time each answer and write down the first thing she taps.

  1. "How much is a semipermanente with nail art?" Pass: says 30 € in under 20 seconds.
  2. "You want to book it for Saturday. Do it." Pass: WhatsApp opens with a message already written, in two taps or fewer.
  3. "Is she open right now? And on Monday morning?" Pass: both answers correct in under 20 seconds.
  4. "Is there an offer you would take?" Pass: she names one offer and its price without scrolling back and forth.
  5. Take the phone away. "What is this place, where is it, and what would you tell a friend about her?" Pass: she says beauty salon, Cittadella, and one thing about Denise herself.

Target: four of five clients pass each question. Any question that two clients fail is a section to fix before launch. Run it on the mockup you choose, and again on the finished site.
